Career Roadmap

The Path to Becoming a Doctor

Every journey is different, but these are the major stages students generally follow when preparing for a career as a physician.

High School

Build a strong foundation in biology, chemistry, mathematics, communication, and service.

Undergraduate Study

Complete a bachelor's degree and the required science courses for medical school admission.

Medical School

Study medical sciences, complete clinical rotations, and develop practical patient-care skills.

Residency

Receive supervised professional training in a chosen medical specialty.

Medical Practice

Become licensed, continue professional learning, and begin serving patients and communities.

Financial Support

Scholarship Opportunities

The cost of higher education should not prevent talented students from pursuing careers in medicine. Students should explore scholarships offered by colleges, hospitals, community organizations, foundations, and professional medical associations.

Useful scholarship categories include undergraduate scholarships, medical school scholarships, community-based awards, and programs designed to support students from underrepresented backgrounds.
